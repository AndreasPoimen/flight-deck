About the data

Companies and their sites are separate records. Subsidiaries and specialist business units can have their own entry. A company may have more offices than those currently mapped.

Source links and review notes appear in company details. A reviewed website confirms relevant public information, not that every role, headcount or address is current. Unconfirmed sites are clearly marked.

Coordinates are approximate city or campus centres unless a site-specific position is available. They are for finding employers, not navigation. The globe uses Natural Earth boundaries (1:110m while moving; 1:50m when still); Detailed map uses OpenStreetMap street tiles.

Size bands retained from the original directory are unverified. New entries use “Unknown” when a headcount has not been sourced. “Startup / scaleup” is an editorial discovery category, not a verified legal status.

Public job feeds load on selection or Refresh. Other companies have official websites and careers/contact routes. No feed does not mean no jobs. We do not send messages or promise replies.

Company density counts distinct company entries in fixed 2° latitude / longitude cells. Several sites of one company in a cell count once. Subsidiaries with separate entries count separately. This is directory coverage, not a map of all industry employment. Cells vary in area by latitude.
